# Palettes

Keep the background black. Swap the accent pack to suit the concept. Pure black #000000 is also supported; keep any colored wash localized around elements.

## Shared

```
--void: #07070C
--void-wash: #0B0614
--icon-body: #12121A
--icon-edge: #1C1C28
--white: #F5F3FF
--check-white: #FFFFFF
```

Optional orbit stroke (only for orbital layouts) = accent mid at 0.28 alpha.
Optional podium inner wash (only when using a podium) = accent deep at 0.45 alpha, blurred.
Bloom = accent mid, blur 40–80px, opacity 0.4–0.7.

## Purple (default — matches reference)

```
--mid: #8B5CF6
--hot: #C4B5FD
--deep: #4C1D95
--emissive: #A78BFA
```

Use for Python, AI/ML platform, general engineering brand, any “match the uploaded cover” request.


Use for security, incident response, reliability risk, threat modeling. Do not use blood/gore materials. Same lacquer language as purple.

## Mixing ban

One pack per frame. A red shield hero does not get a green chart and a blue database. Recolor every accent face, badge, orbit, and bloom to the chosen pack.

## Badge treatment

Badges are optional and must communicate a relevant state. A check badge may use a small disc of `--mid` with a white check. No fixed badge count or satellite layout is required.
